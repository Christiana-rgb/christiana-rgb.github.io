About

Christiana Sia James is a Sierra Leonean entrepreneur and the founder of Amaris, a period-care and social-impact brand committed to making menstrual hygiene products more accessible to women and girls.

Her entrepreneurial journey began on March 28, 2025, with the establishment of The Luxe Shoppe, a business that later evolved into Amaris following its rebranding in 2026.

Today, she continues to develop Amaris as a brand that combines entrepreneurship, menstrual health advocacy and social responsibility.

Amaris and the fight against period poverty

Christiana saw the challenges women and girls face in getting essential menstrual hygiene products, and gave her brand a deeper mission: helping girls who cannot afford sanitary products.

1,000+sanitary products donated to girls in need through community donations and outreach.

Her vision is a society where no girl has to miss school, compromise her comfort or sacrifice her dignity because she cannot afford menstrual hygiene products.

Amaris was founded by Christiana Sia James and established on March 28, 2025, as The Luxe Shoppe. It was rebranded in 2026.
